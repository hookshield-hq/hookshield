# Refund Policy

This policy covers payments for **HookShield Cloud** (the Pro and Business plans and their extra requests). The open-source software is free and has nothing to refund.

## Who sells and refunds

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller **Paddle.com**. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders: it charges your payment method, issues the invoice and processes refunds. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

## 14-day refund

You can request a **full refund within 14 days** of any payment — the first subscription payment, a renewal, or a charge for extra requests. No reason is needed.

## How to ask

- Reply to the receipt Paddle emailed you, or look up your order at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net); or
- write to **hello@hookshield.dev** from your account's email address, with the date of the payment.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

## Cancelling

Cancelling a subscription (Billing → **Manage billing** in the dashboard) stops future renewals; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period. Cancelling is separate from a refund: to get money back for a period already paid, ask for a refund as above. When you get a refund for the current period, the subscription is cancelled too, so it does not renew.

## Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any right you have under the consumer law of your country.
